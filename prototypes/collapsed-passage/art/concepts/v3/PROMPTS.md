# 수녀 SD 시안 — v3

## gameplay-concept-auto-sd-v3

사용자가 전투 전체 자동 진행을 선택했다. SD 화면은 관전·일시정지·배속만 보여주고, 수동 카드 예약·실행 버튼은 사용하지 않는다. SD 캐릭터는 수녀와 동료 모두 몸을 덮는 의상으로 통일한다.

Use case: stylized-concept and ui-mockup.
Asset type: SD DARK-FANTASY FULLY AUTOMATIC BATTLE gameplay concept for "붕괴 통로", wide landscape 16:9.
Input roles: image 1 is the new modest SD NUN and is the strict character/clothing/proportion/style reference. Image 2 provides the female guard's red-ponytail identity and baton/shield only; redesign her into a FULLY CLOTHED modest SD uniform to match image 1. Image 3 provides the broad bald-crown ordinary shield convict. Image 4 provides the unique tall chained male pursuer.
All characters represent adults; this is a NON-SEXUAL cute SD interpretation. Do not copy the prior revealing corset/maid-like fashion onto any SD figure.

Characters: exactly TWO friendly characters on the LEFT foreground, the exact adult SD nun age 25 from image 1 with black religious veil, ivory face-framing wimple and chest/shoulder guimpe, long-sleeved modest black-violet mid-calf habit/scapular, rope belt, boots, round candle-seal buckler and little wax-candle lantern; and ONE SD adult female guard age 28 with wine-red high ponytail, confident mature face, long-sleeved charcoal-violet high-collared guard coat, plain waist belt, dark trousers, practical boots, baton and rectangular shield. Nun and guard are both about 3.5 heads tall with large expressive eyes and clearly separate hands/boots. No cleavage, corsets, bare upper thighs, maid aprons, lingerie or high heels.
On the RIGHT foreground a broad adult male escaped convict with bald crown, short side hair, coarse prison tunic, red waist cloth, scavenged shoulder plate and battered rectangular shield, in a 3.5-head compact threatening SD design.
In a DEEP LEFT REAR doorway, the unique human male condemned prisoner whom the nun prayed for slowly approaches, shaved head, gaunt face, long prisoner tunic, wrist shackles and dragging chain. He is a separate persistent pursuer and NOT an ally or ordinary combat enemy. A slightly taller 4.5-head SD silhouette makes him ominous without gore or monstrous transformation.

Scene: cold gothic underground prison of old stone, broken bars framing the margins, purple-black shadow depths, low warm candles, pale escape light beyond the foreground enemy on the right. Cute readable characters against threatening dark architecture, wide clear center floor, no dark fog over hands/heads/legs. The nun is the first focal point. One lilac guard impact or small healing glow may show AUTOMATIC ACTION in progress.
Style: match image 1 precisely, polished 2D SD gothic fantasy, clean tapered ink contours, clear cel-shaded rounded forms, delicate painted texture, ivory cloth and warm candle against deep violet. Not realistic or 3D.

GAMEPLAY: THE WHOLE FIGHT IS AUTOMATIC. The player watches battles; they choose deck rewards, companions and route BETWEEN fights. The concept must require no selecting targets, arranging cards, reserving actions, pressing an execute button or ending turns.
Upper 72%: automatic combat stage, full visible SD bodies and tiny simple HP bars. Top a subtle pursuit strip with ONE shackled silhouette approaching a lantern symbol.
Lower 28%: compact OBSERVATION PANEL. A row of five illustrated cards shows the current automatically used deck hand as noninteractive spectator information, with small costs. Above this a small latest-action strip can show nun portrait -> shield -> guard portrait. On the RIGHT only two compact spectator controls: "일시정지" and "2×". Heading reads EXACTLY "자동 전투". Title top-left reads EXACTLY "붕괴 통로". No other lettering or fake text.
Do NOT include an action-reservation queue, manual target crosshairs, an "execute" button or a "turn end" button. The currently played card can be subtly highlighted automatically. Card illustrations show lantern, round shield, bandage, baton and short chain. Keep bottom cards completely within the frame.
Constraints: one nun, one female guard, one ordinary foreground convict, one separate rear pursuer. Preserve roles and faces from references while converting all into coherent SD forms. Nun must read instantly as a NUN, not a maid. Safe modest SD character design, no erotic poses, nudity or gore. This is an art proposal, not the current application's screenshot.

사용자 피드백: SD 스타일 검토, v2 의상은 수녀보다 메이드처럼 보임. 이번 시안은 성인 수녀의 비성적인 SD 디자인으로, 실제 베일·윔플·어깨천·수도복·스카풀라 실루엣을 우선한다. 약 3.5등신, 큰 눈·작은 방패·등불·부츠로 귀여움을 살린다.

도구: built-in `image_gen`. 참조: v1 수녀의 정체성과 도구, v2의 채색만 사용. 원화·전투 적용과는 별도 시안이다.

## nun-concept-sd-v3

Use case: stylized-concept / character redesign.
Asset type: SUPER-DEFORMED SD NUN character-design study for the Korean dark-fantasy game "붕괴 통로".
Input references: image 1 is the ORIGINAL modest nun design and her lantern/buckler, which establishes her identity and recognizably religious clothing. Image 2 is a prior ornate gothic fashion iteration that the user specifically rejected because it looks like a MAID. Retain only its polished expressive 2D coloring, NOT its clothing.
Primary request: a cute SD interpretation of a clearly ADULT nun age 25. Approximately 3.5-head-tall compact figure, large head and expressive amber eyes, mature adult face cues and composed adult demeanor. Cute rounded silhouette, short ink-black bob barely visible at the brow, gentle determined expression. Fully clothed, non-sexual SD design with no cleavage or exposed upper thighs. This is a capable nun who protects companions and escapes a dangerous prison.

MAKE HER READ AS A NUN, NOT A MAID:
- A genuine long BLACK RELIGIOUS VEIL over an IVORY WIMPLE that frames her face and covers her neck.
- A broad simple IVORY GUIMPE / SHOULDER YOKE covering the chest and shoulders.
- A long-sleeved BLACK-VIOLET HABIT with a broad simple SCAPULAR hanging straight down the front.
- A plain rope cincture and a small rosary at the waist.
- Mid-calf hem with practical lace-up boots and clearly separate small hands; small expressive sleeves, no puff-shoulder frills.
- Her characteristic small round iron-rimmed wooden buckler with a simple wax-candle seal, and little wax-candle lantern.
- No maid headband, apron, corset, cleavage, garter belt, layered ruffled miniskirt, fishnets, high heels or lingerie. Do not copy the prior V2 costume.
Cuteness comes from the large head, round shield, little heavy boots, alert eyebrows and warm lantern. Use restrained silver fastenings and one small religious cross, not decorative gold crosses all over the outfit.

Style: original 2D gothic dark-fantasy game SD, bold clean tapered outlines, clear flat silhouette, polished cel shading and gentle painted texture, deep violet shadows, warm ivory cloth, amber candle glow. Dark prison-world costume wear appears subtly at hem and boots, while the face remains expressive and charming. This is a readable small combat character rather than an ornate collectible fashion illustration.

Layout: a wide 3:2 pale-gray/lilac concept board. One LARGE full-body three-quarter SD view on the LEFT, front/side/back reference views at the TOP RIGHT, 3 expressions at the BOTTOM RIGHT (determined, startled, reassured smile), plus small lantern/shield and veil-wimple breakdown studies. Same adult nun, clothes and 3.5-head proportions in all views. All feet, veil and equipment completely visible. Neutral study lighting, not a scene.
Text (verbatim): "수녀 · SD 시안" only. No watermark or extra writing.
Constraints: distinctly a religious nun on every view, appropriate for cute dark fantasy; avoid turning her into a maid, witch, child or schoolgirl. No erotic pose, nudity, gore or extra characters.
